Fresh from Our Farm to Your Family

Welcome to Green Valley Dairy Farm, where three generations of the Henderson family have been producing the finest dairy products in the region. Our commitment to sustainable farming practices and animal welfare ensures that every product we create meets the highest standards of quality and freshness.

Experience the difference that comes from pasture-raised cows, traditional methods, and genuine care for the land.

Our Story

A Legacy of Quality Since 1952

Green Valley Dairy Farm was founded by Robert Henderson in 1952 with just 12 dairy cows and a dream of providing the community with fresh, wholesome milk. Today, his grandchildren continue that tradition on our 200-acre farm, where over 100 happy, healthy cows graze on lush pastures and produce the milk that goes into all our products.

Our Values

We believe in doing things the right way, not the easy way. Our cows are pasture-raised and grass-fed, spending their days outdoors in the fresh air and sunshine. We never use artificial growth hormones, and we work closely with local veterinarians to ensure the health and wellbeing of our herd.

Sustainability is at the heart of everything we do. We use renewable energy to power our operations, practice rotational grazing to maintain healthy soil, and recycle all our water. Our commitment to the environment ensures that Green Valley will continue to thrive for generations to come.

Community Connection

As a family-owned business, we're deeply connected to our community. We partner with local schools for educational farm tours, support regional food banks with product donations, and source our supplies from neighboring businesses whenever possible. When you choose Green Valley, you're supporting not just our farm, but the entire local economy.

Our Products

Fresh Whole Milk

Our signature whole milk is creamy, rich, and full of natural goodness. Bottled within hours of milking, it retains all the nutrients and flavor that make farm-fresh milk special. Available in glass bottles or recyclable containers.

  • Non-homogenized with cream on top
  • No artificial hormones or antibiotics
  • Available in whole, 2%, and skim varieties
  • Half-gallon and gallon sizes

Artisan Cheese

Handcrafted in small batches using traditional methods, our cheese selection includes aged cheddar, creamy brie, tangy blue cheese, and fresh mozzarella. Each variety is carefully aged to perfection in our temperature-controlled cheese cave.

  • Award-winning aged cheddar (12-month and 24-month)
  • Soft-ripened brie and camembert
  • Fresh mozzarella made daily
  • Seasonal specialty varieties

Cultured Butter

Made from cultured cream and churned in small batches, our butter has a rich, complex flavor that elevates any dish. We offer both salted and unsalted varieties, perfect for cooking, baking, or simply spreading on fresh bread.

  • European-style cultured butter
  • Higher butterfat content for superior flavor
  • Salted and unsalted options
  • Available in 8oz and 1lb packages

Greek Yogurt

Thick, creamy, and packed with protein, our Greek yogurt is strained using traditional methods to achieve the perfect texture. Made with live active cultures and available in plain or with locally-sourced fruit preserves.

  • High protein, low sugar
  • Live and active cultures
  • Plain, vanilla, and fruit varieties
  • Individual cups and family-size containers

Heavy Cream

Our heavy cream is perfect for whipping, cooking, or adding richness to your coffee. With a high butterfat content and no additives, it whips up beautifully and adds luxurious texture to any recipe.

  • 40% butterfat content
  • No stabilizers or thickeners
  • Perfect for whipping and cooking
  • Pint and quart sizes

Seasonal Specialties

Throughout the year, we offer limited-edition products that celebrate the seasons. From pumpkin spice yogurt in fall to fresh strawberry ice cream in summer, our seasonal offerings showcase the best of what each time of year has to offer.

  • Small-batch ice cream (summer)
  • Flavored milk varieties
  • Holiday gift baskets
  • Limited-edition cheese flavors

Find Our Products

In addition to our farm store, you can find Green Valley Dairy products at farmers' markets throughout the region and at select local grocery stores and co-ops. Visit our website or call us for a complete list of retail locations.

Farm Tours

We offer guided farm tours every Saturday at 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM from May through October. Tours last approximately 90 minutes and include a visit to the milking parlor, a walk through the pastures, and a tasting of our products. Reservations are required and can be made by calling or emailing us.
