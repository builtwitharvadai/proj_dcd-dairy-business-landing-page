Welcome to Green Valley Dairy

Experience the pure taste of farm-fresh dairy products, delivered straight from our pastures to your table. Our family-owned dairy has been serving the community with premium quality milk, cheese, and dairy products for over three generations.

Where tradition meets quality in every drop

About Our Dairy

Our Story

Green Valley Dairy has been a cornerstone of our community since 1952. What started as a small family farm with just a handful of cows has grown into a thriving dairy operation, while still maintaining the personal touch and quality standards that our grandparents established.

Our Commitment

We believe in sustainable farming practices and treating our animals with the utmost care and respect. Our cows graze on lush, pesticide-free pastures, and we never use artificial growth hormones. This commitment to natural, ethical farming results in the freshest, most flavorful dairy products you'll find anywhere.

Quality You Can Trust

Every product that leaves our farm meets the highest standards of quality and freshness. We process our milk daily, ensuring that what reaches your table is as fresh as possible. Our state-of-the-art facilities combined with time-honored techniques create dairy products that are both safe and delicious.
