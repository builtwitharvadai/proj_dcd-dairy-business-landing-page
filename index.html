Our Story

A Legacy of Quality Since 1985

Green Valley Dairy began as a small family farm with a simple mission: to provide our community with the freshest, highest-quality dairy products possible. For nearly four decades, we've stayed true to that commitment.

Our Values

We believe in sustainable farming practices that respect our animals, our land, and our customers. Our cows graze on lush, pesticide-free pastures, and we never use artificial growth hormones or unnecessary antibiotics.

Community First

As a local business, we're proud to support our community by sourcing locally, employing local workers, and giving back through various charitable initiatives. When you choose Green Valley Dairy, you're supporting sustainable agriculture and local families.

Our Premium Dairy Products

Fresh Whole Milk

Creamy, full-bodied milk from grass-fed cows. Available in whole, 2%, 1%, and skim varieties. Delivered fresh daily in recyclable glass bottles.

  • 100% grass-fed cows
  • No artificial hormones
  • Rich in vitamins and minerals
  • Available in multiple fat percentages

Artisan Cheese Collection

Handcrafted cheeses aged to perfection. From sharp cheddar to creamy brie, our master cheesemakers create exceptional flavors using traditional methods.

  • Aged cheddar (mild to extra sharp)
  • Creamy mozzarella
  • Gourmet brie and camembert
  • Specialty seasonal varieties

Organic Yogurt

Smooth, probiotic-rich yogurt made with organic milk and live active cultures. Available in plain, vanilla, and seasonal fruit flavors.

  • Live probiotic cultures
  • No artificial sweeteners
  • Low-fat and full-fat options
  • Greek and traditional styles

Farm Fresh Butter

Rich, golden butter churned from the cream of grass-fed cows. Perfect for cooking, baking, or spreading on your morning toast.

  • Salted and unsalted varieties
  • High butterfat content
  • Made from cultured cream
  • Available in bulk for bakeries

Heavy Cream

Premium heavy cream perfect for whipping, cooking, and coffee. Our cream is pasteurized but not ultra-pasteurized to preserve natural flavor.

  • 36% butterfat minimum
  • No stabilizers or additives
  • Perfect for whipping
  • Ideal for gourmet cooking

Cottage Cheese

Fresh, creamy cottage cheese with large or small curds. High in protein and perfect for healthy snacking or cooking.

  • Large and small curd options
  • Low-fat and full-fat varieties
  • No artificial preservatives
  • High protein content
